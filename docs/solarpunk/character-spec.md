# 3D Asset Specification: Solarpunk Explorer (Main Character)

Reference image: `image_20261107_212618.jpg` (saved alongside this spec).

## 1. Core Identity & Art Direction
- **Archetype:** High-speed eco-explorer and pilot.
- **Art Style:** High-fidelity stylized anime. Designed with clean, bold forms to stand out in high contrast against hyper-realistic, earth-scale environments.
- **Proportions:** Stylized "chibi-adjacent" (roughly 4.5 to 5 heads tall). Larger head for expressive readability; oversized, chunky gear (boots, backpack) to ground the character and emphasize physical impact during high-speed movement.

## 2. Anatomy & Facial Features
- **Head & Hair:** Voluminous, textured dark-brown/black hair with natural, wind-swept layers.
- **Ears:** Prominent, dark-furred feline (nekomimi) ears emerging from the crown of the hair. Must be rigged for emotive twitching and wind reaction.
- **Face:** Soft, rounded features with a minimal nose and mouth. Oversized, highly expressive amber/brown eyes with detailed, luminous irises and thick eyelashes.
- **Left Arm (Cybernetic):** A sleek, white synthetic prosthetic arm. Smooth, seamless paneling with inset emissive channels displaying flowing, light-blue/cyan energy patterns in a curvilinear, tech-tribal aesthetic.

## 3. Apparel & Material Setup
- **Base Suit:** Loose-fitting, off-white/light beige utility flight suit. Durable, matte synthetic canvas (ripstop / high-tech nylon) with slight bunching at the joints.
- **Accents & Strapping:** Sage/mint green tactical webbing, harnesses, and buckles across chest, waist, and thighs to break up the silhouette.
- **Footwear:** Oversized, chunky white utility boots with thick impact-absorbing soles, green strap accents, and a prominent 'X' mechanical hinge or decal on the outer ankles.
- **Armor:** Hard-shell white circular knee pads and reinforced collar padding.

## 4. Specialized Gear & Attachments
- **Flight Backpack (Solarpunk Jetpack):**
  - Chassis: blocky, modular white and mint-green tech unit.
  - Wings: short, deployable winglets with embedded blue solar grids on top surfaces.
  - Terrarium Module: transparent, illuminated capsule built into the lower back of the pack, housing a live green sprout/plant.
- **Flight Goggles:** Thick, white-framed goggles with sky-blue tinted lenses. Rigged as a separate mesh to toggle between resting on the forehead/neck and actively worn over the eyes during supersonic travel.

## 5. Technical & Rigging Implementation
- **Rigging:** Standard humanoid skeleton with supplementary bone chains for the cat ears, hair dynamics, backpack wing deployment, and outfit straps.
- **Shading:** Stylized PBR. Requires emissive map support for the cybernetic arm patterns and backpack terrarium lighting. Material values must stay highly readable and vibrant across drastically different biome lighting scenarios.
- **Physics & Dynamics:** Physics proxies (jiggle bones / cloth simulation) for the hair tufts, backpack plant, and loose straps to react dynamically to supersonic flight, freefalling, and high-speed wind resistance.
